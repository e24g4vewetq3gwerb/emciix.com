How it works

For car owners

  1. Post your car

    Pick the service (exterior, interior, full detail, snow or ice), add photos and suggest a price.

  2. Compare offers

    Nearby ID-verified washers accept your price or counter. See ratings and chat before you book.

  3. Book in seconds

    Confirm one offer. Your exact spot is shared only with the washer you booked.

  4. Track, pay, rate

    Follow their arrival live, get before and after photos, pay the washer directly and rate them.

Get started

For washers

  1. Verify your ID

    A quick ID and selfie check keeps owners and washers safe.

  2. Set your radius

    Choose your services and how far you travel (3 to 25 km). Only requests inside it show up.

  3. Accept or counter

    Take the price or send a counter offer. No fees for washers, ever.

  4. Navigate and get paid

    Open directions in Google Maps or Apple Maps, do the job, keep 100% of your price.
