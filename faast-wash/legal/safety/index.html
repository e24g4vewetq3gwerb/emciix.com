Community & Safety Guidelines

Faast Wash, operated by EMCIIX (Ontario, Canada) · Last updated October 1, 2026

Faast Wash works because Owners and Washers can trust each other. These guidelines apply to everyone; breaking them can lead to removal from Faast Wash.

Everyone

Be respectful. No harassment, threats, hate or discrimination of any kind.

Keep communication in the app until a booking is confirmed (contact details are blocked in chat before booking).

Report anything unsafe or inappropriate with the flag button on a profile, message or job, and block anyone you don’t want to hear from. We aim to review reports within 24 hours.

In an emergency, call 911 first.

Owners

Describe the car and its location accurately, and make sure it is parked where a wash is allowed (ask your building or lot if unsure).

Remove valuables before an interior service and only give access you are comfortable with.

Pay the agreed price directly to the Washer when the job is done, and confirm payment in the app.

Washers

Complete identity verification and keep your insurance current.

Do only the services agreed, use products safe for the vehicle and the surroundings (Faast Wash favours waterless washing), and respect local by-laws and property rules.

Never enter a vehicle without the Owner’s permission. Take the before and after photos the app asks for.

If you can’t make it, cancel as early as possible. Missing a booking (15 minutes past your arrival estimate without arriving) results in a strike.

Winter

Snow and ice services: watch for traffic and ice underfoot, never use hot water on glass, and don’t block driveways or sidewalks with cleared snow.

Enforcement

Depending on severity we may warn, remove content, suspend or permanently ban an account, and we cooperate with police when required by law.
