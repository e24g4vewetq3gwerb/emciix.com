1. What Faast Wash is

Faast Wash is a marketplace that connects car owners ("Owners") with independent car washers ("Washers") who come to where the car is parked.

EMCIIX is not a car-wash company and does not perform washes. Washers are independent businesses, not employees, agents or contractors of EMCIIX. The wash itself is a contract between the Owner and the Washer.

2. Accounts and eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old and able to enter a binding contract in Ontario. Keep your login details private; you are responsible for activity on your account.

Washers must complete identity verification (ID document and selfie, processed by our verification provider) before they can go online or send offers, and confirm that they carry insurance appropriate for the work they do.

3. Prices, payment and the booking fee

Owners suggest a price; Washers can accept it or counter. The agreed wash price is paid by the Owner directly to the Washer (for example cash, Interac e-Transfer or tap), and Washers keep 100% of it. EMCIIX does not collect or hold the wash price.

When an Owner confirms a booking, EMCIIX charges the Owner a booking fee: currently $2.99 CAD, or $4.99 CAD for a full detail. Every 10th completed booking is fee-free. Washers are never charged a fee by EMCIIX.

Taxes: the booking fee is a taxable supply. Where EMCIIX is registered for GST/HST, 13% Ontario HST applies to the booking fee and will be shown on your receipt. Washers are responsible for their own income tax and, if they are registrants (for example above the $30,000 small-supplier threshold), for charging and remitting GST/HST on their wash price.

Booking fees are processed by our payment provider (Stripe). Card details are handled by Stripe and are never stored by EMCIIX.

4. Cancellations and refunds of the booking fee

Owner cancels before the Washer is on the way: booking fee refunded. Owner cancels after the Washer is on the way: no refund.

Washer cancels, or does not arrive within 15 minutes of the estimated arrival time: booking fee refunded and the Washer receives a strike.

Disputes about the quality of a wash or damage are between the Owner and the Washer; you can report a problem in the app and we will review it, and we may refund the booking fee where a complaint is upheld. Your rights under the Ontario Consumer Protection Act, 2002 are not affected.

5. Your conduct

Be honest, respectful and safe. Do not harass, threaten or discriminate against anyone (the Ontario Human Rights Code applies), post illegal or offensive content, misrepresent a vehicle or service, or use Faast Wash for anything other than arranging car washes.

Before a booking is confirmed, chat filters block phone numbers, emails, links and social handles so that bookings stay in the app.

Washers must only perform the services agreed, use products that are safe for the vehicle and the location, follow local by-laws, and not enter a vehicle without the Owner’s permission.

6. Ratings, reports and moderation
