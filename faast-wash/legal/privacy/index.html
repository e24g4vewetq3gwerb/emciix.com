Privacy Policy

Faast Wash, operated by EMCIIX (Ontario, Canada) · Last updated October 1, 2026

This policy explains what personal information EMCIIX collects through Faast Wash, why, and your choices. We follow the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). Our privacy contact is reachable at contact@emciix.com.

1. What we collect

Account: we do not ask for, or store in our database, your name, email address, phone number, photo or a password. You sign in with Apple, Google or X, and we keep only a provider account ID (which links that sign-in to a random Faast Wash user ID) and a generated display handle (for example "Owner 1093"), plus your role (Owner/Washer), language and the booking data below. We request the least each provider allows: no name or email scopes from Apple, no extra scopes from Google beyond what sign-in itself requires, and none from X.

Your sign-in provider (Apple, Google or X) holds your email address and other account details under its own privacy policy. To complete a sign-in, our authentication service (Google Firebase Authentication) receives what the provider supplies: always a provider account ID and, depending on the provider and your settings, your email address, name and profile photo. Firebase Authentication may retain these internally as part of your login record. We clear the name and photo from your login profile, never copy any of it into our database, logs or notifications, never show it to other users and never use it to contact you. It is deleted with your login when you delete your account.

Vehicles: make, model, colour, size and optional plate.

Requests and jobs: service, price, time, the car’s exact location, address and notes, photos of the car (before/after), job status history, ratings and comments.

Location: when you choose "Use my location" while booking; for Washers who are online, an approximate position (rounded to about 300 m) so Owners can see roughly where Washers are; during an active job, the Washer’s exact position, shared only with that job’s Owner. Location is used only while the app is open; Faast Wash never tracks location in the background.

Messages between an Owner and a Washer about a request, and reports and blocks you make.

Washer verification: our provider (Stripe Identity) collects an ID document and selfie to verify identity; we receive the result (verified or not), not copies of your ID.

Payments: our provider (Stripe) processes card payments for booking fees; we receive the payment status and amount, not your full card number.

Device: push notification token (if you allow notifications), app preferences (such as your navigation app), and basic technical logs for security and troubleshooting (these never include your email address or name).

2. Why we use it

To run the marketplace: show requests to nearby Washers, send offers, confirm bookings, share the exact location with the booked Washer, show live arrival, and process booking fees and refunds.

To keep people safe: identity verification, chat filtering before booking, reports, blocks and moderation, fraud prevention.

To send notifications you asked for (new offers, messages, job updates) and to provide support.

We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Who we share it with

The other party to your booking: Washers see an approximate area before booking and the exact location, address and notes after the booking is confirmed; Owners see the Washer’s display handle, rating and, during the job, live location. Neither side sees the other’s email address or real name.

Service providers acting for us: Apple, Google and X (sign-in only, as described above), Google Firebase (authentication, database, file storage, server functions), Stripe (payments and identity verification), Expo (push notification delivery), Vercel (website hosting), and OpenStreetMap services on the website (map tiles and address lookup).

Some providers store or process data outside Canada, including in the United States (for example our main database is in a US multi-region; server functions and photo storage are in Montréal). Information stored abroad may be accessible to authorities there under local law.

Authorities, when required by law or to protect someone’s safety.

4. How long we keep it

Your profile, vehicles, open requests and photos, and your login record (including anything Firebase Authentication kept from your sign-in provider), are deleted when you delete your account. Messages you sent are replaced with "[deleted]".

Records of completed jobs, ratings and booking-fee payments are kept, without your profile, for disputes and as required by Canadian tax law (generally six years).

5. Your choices and rights

You can access and correct your profile in the app, turn notifications off in Settings, deny or revoke location permission in your device settings (you can still type an address), and delete your account in Settings > Delete account.

Under PIPEDA you can ask for access to, or correction of, the personal information we hold about you, and withdraw consent (which may mean we can no longer provide the service). Email contact@emciix.com; we respond within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca).

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit; access is limited by role-based rules (for example, only the booked Washer can read a car’s exact location). If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify affected people and the Privacy Commissioner as PIPEDA requires.

7. Website storage

The website uses your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in and remember preferences (language, map style, navigation app). We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

8. Children

Faast Wash is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

9. Changes

We will post updates here and tell you in the app about material changes. Last updated October 1, 2026.
