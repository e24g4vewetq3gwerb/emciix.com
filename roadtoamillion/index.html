Road to a Million: a money game you can learn from.

Start with 1,000 and see how many months it takes to reach 1,000,000. Each turn is one month: split your pay between pots, handle life events, and buy upgrades. Free, offline, with no ads, accounts or in-app purchases.

Features

How it plays

All names, rates and companies in the game are made up. It's a game for learning, not financial advice.

One month per turn

Split your pay between pots like Snugpot (safe), Growvine (grows faster) and Hustlepatch (raises your pay). Zoomzap, Bossnest and Rentroost unlock as your money grows.

Life events

Quakes, surprise bills, windfalls and big choices where you decide what to do.

22 upgrades

Earn points and spend them on upgrades, and move through life stages from Sproutling to Firstgig and beyond.

Learn tab

8 short lessons with quick checks, scenarios, a tap-to-define glossary, and a practice market with fictional companies and pretend money.

Watch & learn

The game plays a practice run for you. Pick Careful, Balanced or Bold, pause or step through, and see why each move was made.

How to play guide

A built-in guide (the "?" button), where you can also rename the game's words and turn on "Show everything now".

Classroom tools

A shared class code gives everyone the same months, plus a printable worksheet and classroom kit.

Private by design

Works fully offline. No accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, and nothing leaves your device.
