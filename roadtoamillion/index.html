Coming to foldable iPhone

Foldable layout concept, coming soon Design concept

A preview of how Road to a Million could adapt to a foldable phone. Folded, it plays like it does on any phone. Unfolded, the larger screen splits in two: the game, chart and activity feed on one side, and Split, Upgrades, Learn and Coach tabs on the other. Progress carries over every time you fold or unfold.

Concept preview (42 s, with sound effects; unmute to listen). This layout is not in the app yet, and the device shown is a generic foldable frame.
