Road to a Million

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026 · Developer: EMCIIX INC · Contact: contact@emciix.com

Road to a Million (“the app”) is an offline money-growth game and personal savings planner made by EMCIIX INC. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: the app does not collect any data about you.

Summary

  • No account or sign-in. The app never asks for your name, email or phone number.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising identifiers.
  • No data is sent to EMCIIX INC or to anyone else. The game and planner run entirely on your device.

Information you enter

Numbers you type into the planner (savings, debts, budget categories, income) and your game progress, scores and settings are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. They never leave your device. You can erase them at any time by using the reset options in the app or by deleting the app.

In-app purchase

The optional “Unlock $1M Goal” purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. EMCIIX INC does not receive your payment details or Apple ID. The app only checks with Apple whether the purchase is active on your device. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Not financial advice

Rates and outcomes in the app are fictional or illustrative and are not financial advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
