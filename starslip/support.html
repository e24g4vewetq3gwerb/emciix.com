FAQ

How do I steer?
Tap or drag the sky to turn. Tap the center to lock your course. The PORTAL panel at the bottom has Speed and Stars sliders, Boost, and more controls under “More” (Roll, Nova, Tunnel, Coast, Save and Load).
How do I open the game?
Tap the planet, then “Play game”. The rhythm game has 24 levels: tap the lanes or the A, SYNC and B pads in time with the notes. Points from your best scores buy upgrades. Tap “✕ PORTAL” to go back to the starfield.
How do I add or change planets?
Tap the planet (or the small “+”) and choose “Add planet”, then pick an app or paste a link. Up to 8 planets orbit the center. Long-press a planet to open, edit or remove it. Links open in Safari or in the linked app.
Do I need an account or an internet connection?
No. There is no sign-in, and the starfield and the game work offline.
Where is my progress saved? Can I move it to another device?
Your scores, upgrades, planets and settings are saved only on your device. They aren’t synced, so they don’t carry over to another device, and deleting the app erases them.
There’s no sound.
Check that your device isn’t in Silent mode and the volume is up, and that the in-app mute buttons (the speaker icon in the PORTAL panel, or MUTE in the game) are off.
Does Starslip collect my data?
No. See the Starslip Privacy Policy.
